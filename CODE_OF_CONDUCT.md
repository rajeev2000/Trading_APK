# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Trading_APK project aims to provide a respectful, constructive environment for everyone who participates in discussions, issues, reviews, or contributions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and listen to other viewpoints.
- Keep feedback relevant to the project and welcome people with different experience levels.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, personal data, or private financial information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and deliberately sharing private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting technical details publicly. The maintainer may remove content, request changes, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
