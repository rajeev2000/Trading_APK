# Code of Conduct

We aim to keep project collaboration respectful, constructive and focused on the work.

Expected behaviour: be respectful and specific in discussions and reviews, explain disagreements with evidence and alternatives, accept constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, insults, discrimination and sharing private information.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, personal attacks, doxxing or publishing credentials/private data.

Contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.
